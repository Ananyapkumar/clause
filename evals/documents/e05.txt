VANTAGE ARCHITECTURAL LIGHTING
Technical Specification


ORION LINEAR RECESSED — 1200mm
Order code as supplied:  VA-ORL-1200-35-90-DALI


Recessed linear luminaire for plasterboard and grid ceilings.
Extruded aluminium housing with opal or microprismatic diffuser.


ORDER CODE STRUCTURE
-------------------------------------------------------------
VA-ORL-[length]-[CCT]-[CRI]-[control]

  length    1200 = 1200mm
  CCT       27 = 2700K   30 = 3000K   35 = 3500K   40 = 4000K
  CRI       80 = Ra80    90 = Ra90
  control   ON/OFF | DALI | 010V


PHOTOMETRIC DATA — 1200mm, Ra90
-------------------------------------------------------------
  CCT          Luminaire flux      Circuit power     Efficacy
  2700 K          3180 lm              32 W          99 lm/W
  3000 K          3290 lm              32 W         103 lm/W
  3500 K          3410 lm              32 W         107 lm/W
  4000 K          3520 lm              32 W         110 lm/W

Note: Ra80 variants deliver approximately 12% higher flux at the
same circuit power. Figures above are for Ra90 only.
